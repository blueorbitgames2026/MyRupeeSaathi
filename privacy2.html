Information we handle

Information you provide

  • Sign-in details: Your mobile number, any OTP you enter, and information needed to maintain your session.
  • Assessment and service details: If you use these features, we may process your questionnaire answers, details about your work or income, financial and repayment information, payment account details, PAN or other identity information you submit, and images or liveness information provided during identity checks.
  • Contacts you select: If a feature lets you choose a contact, we may receive the name and number you select and the relationship you enter.
  • Support requests: If you contact us, we may process your contact details, message, and any supporting information you send.

Device and usage information

Depending on the feature and permissions, the app may handle device and operating system details, app version, network information, storage and battery status, approximate location, information about apps visible to Android, and app or device identifiers. App interactions, installation attribution, crash reports and push notification identifiers may also be processed.

Call history

Call log access is requested for the voice OTP flow and can also be used when the app submits device information. Call history sent to our backend may include phone numbers, names available in the call log, call type, date and time, duration, and country information. The app reads entries in the device’s call log.

How we use information

  • Send OTPs, verify sign-in, and maintain your account and session.
  • Process information you submit for identity checks, credit assessments, and related requests in the app.
  • Support service security and assess potential fraud or misuse.
  • Deliver service notifications, respond to support requests, diagnose crashes, and understand how the app performs.

Analytics and web content

MyRupeeSaathi uses Firebase services for analytics, crash reporting, and push notifications, and Adjust for installation attribution and measurement. Web pages opened inside the app may use cookies or similar storage to keep a session active or remember preferences. External sites may have their own privacy policies.

Device permissions and your choices

Device information and SMS used for credit assessment

MyRupeeSaathi’s assessment model uses device information and, with your permission, selected SMS records to help estimate your credit limit and evaluate financial risk.

Hardware and app details
Device brand and model, screen size, Android version, and the MyRupeeSaathi app identifier and version.
Memory, storage, and battery
Total and available RAM and storage, battery level, and charging status.
Device settings and identifiers
Language and region settings, Android ID, and the advertising identifier when available.
Network details
Connection type, mobile operator name, and local Wi-Fi IP address when available.
App inventory
Names, package identifiers, versions, and installation and update dates for apps Android makes visible to MyRupeeSaathi.
Approximate location
With your permission, the app may obtain Android’s last known network-based location, including approximate latitude and longitude when available. The assessment model uses this information alongside other inputs to help estimate your credit limit and evaluate financial risk.
SMS records selected for financial assessment
With your SMS permission, the app screens messages on your device before upload. The screening looks for financial signals in sender details and message content, such as references to banks, payments, account activity, and credit. These rules can also match other messages. Records that match the screening rules may include sender details, message content, timestamps, and message status. The assessment model uses the selected records to estimate credit limits and evaluate financial risk.

Encrypted upload

Device information, including approximate location when available, and the SMS records selected by this screening are encrypted before upload and sent over HTTPS to https://scrsth5.com. We do not share device information or selected SMS records collected for this assessment with third parties.

Permissions and controls

  • Camera and image selection: Lets you take or select a photo when a feature needs an image or identity check.
  • Approximate location access: Lets the app obtain your approximate location for credit and financial risk assessment.
  • Notifications: Lets the app display service notifications. You can turn them off in Android Settings.
  • SMS access: Lets the app screen messages on your device and upload the selected records for the assessment.
  • Call log access: Required to request an OTP through the voice-call option in this version.

You can review or revoke permissions in Android Settings under Apps → MyRupeeSaathi → Permissions. Turning off a permission may make the related feature unavailable.

Sharing, transfers, and security

Information submitted through the app may be sent to our backend to provide the service. Where needed, service providers may process information to support hosting, OTP delivery, identity verification, analytics, crash reporting, or notifications. If you use in-app liveness verification, the app can open an Accuauth verification flow. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to respond to a security incident or protect legal rights.

Our service and providers may process information in countries other than the country where you use the app. The app uses HTTPS for communications with its configured service backend. Access to app features and device information is also subject to account checks and Android permission controls where applicable.

Retention, deletion, and privacy requests

Retention depends on why the information was collected. Account, service, security, and support records may be kept while needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, investigate misuse, or meet legal obligations.

To request deletion of your account or associated data, or to ask for access to or correction of your information, email blueorbitgames12@gmail.com. Include the mobile number associated with the account so we can identify your request. Please keep OTPs and passwords out of your email. We may ask for information to verify that the request comes from the account holder. Some records may need to be retained where law requires it.

Children and policy updates

MyRupeeSaathi is intended for adults. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review the matter.
