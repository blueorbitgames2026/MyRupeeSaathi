Delete Your Account

Before you begin

If you have an outstanding loan, you must repay it in full before you can delete your account.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the MyRupeeSaathi app and sign in.
  2. Tap Mine, the third tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account and follow the on-screen instructions.

After deletion

When we delete your account, we remove all your personal information from our servers. You can then use the same mobile number to register a new account.
